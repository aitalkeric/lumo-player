隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

Lumo Player 支持

Lumo Player 是连接你自己的媒体服务器的 iPhone 播放器，购买不包含影片内容或服务器账号。

如需帮助，请联系 aitalkeric@gmail.com。可附 App 版本、iOS 版本和问题描述；请勿发送服务器密码、访问令牌或付款卡信息。

购买与订阅

在 App 设置的“无限播放”中恢复购买或管理订阅。终身买断不会自动取消已有月度订阅，请在 Apple 的订阅管理中取消续费。

Support

Lumo Player connects to your own media server. A purchase does not include movies or a server account.

For help, email aitalkeric@gmail.com with your app version, iOS version and a description. Do not send passwords, access tokens or payment-card information.

Restore purchases and manage subscriptions from Unlimited Playback in app settings. Buying lifetime access does not automatically cancel an existing monthly subscription.
