Lumo Player 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 16 日

Lumo Player（以下称“本 App”）由个人开发者提供，即 App Store 页面显示的卖家。本政策说明本 App 处理哪些信息、为什么处理，以及你可以如何控制。

概述

  • 本 App 不要求注册独立的 Lumo 账户。连接个人媒体服务器时，你需要使用该服务器的账号。
  • 当前版本不包含广告、统计分析或跨 App 追踪 SDK，不请求通讯录或地理位置权限。
  • 本 App 不将你的媒体库账号、密码、观看记录或影片资料上传至开发者的数据服务器。

媒体服务器与保存在设备上的信息

登录时，你的设备直接向你填写的媒体服务器发送用户名、密码以及由 App 随机生成的设备标识。后续请求使用服务器提供的访问令牌；播放会话、进度和收藏操作也可能发送给该服务器。服务器运营者可收到你的 IP 地址、请求时间等网络信息，并按其自己的政策处理。

连接地址、账号、访问令牌和可选密码保存在本机 Keychain，用于后续连接，未启用 Keychain 同步。目录、图片缓存、收藏、稍后观看、近期观看和播放偏好保存在设备本地。当前版本未启用个人记录的 iCloud 同步。系统备份是否包含本机数据取决于你的 iOS 备份设置。

图片与名称查询的网络连接

片库和影片资料主要从你连接的媒体服务器读取。当服务器返回 TMDB 的图片地址时，图片页可能直接从 image.tmdb.org 加载图片或缩略图。影片和人物名称匹配功能可能向 Wikidata（www.wikidata.org）发送影片或人物名称、搜索词和语言。相关服务可收到网络连接所必需的 IP 地址等信息，并按各自的隐私政策处理。本 App 不向这些名称或图片服务发送媒体服务器的登录密码。

剪贴板与电视配对

连接流程可能尝试识别剪贴板中的连接文本。你选择“复制连接”时，App 会把连接信息写入系统剪贴板，其中可能包含密码；请仅向你信任的接收方分享。

配对电视时，App 通过加密的本地网络连接，将媒体服务器地址、访问令牌等连接信息发送至你选择的电视。当前配对数据不包含媒体服务器密码。电视遥控连接密钥保存在本机 Keychain。

购买与免费体验

订阅、终身买断、付款及退款由 Apple 处理。本 App 只读取 Apple 验证后的交易和订阅状态以判断无限播放权益，不获取你的支付卡或付款方式。每天的免费体验额度在本机记录日期和累计实际播放时长，并保存在本机 Keychain；此记录不用于广告或跨 App 识别，也不跨设备同步。

设备权限

  • 照片（仅添加）：仅在你选择保存图片时请求添加到相册，不读取已有照片。
  • 相机：用于扫描电视配对二维码，以及在你启用空间追踪时估计手机姿态。当前实现不保存或上传相机画面。
  • 运动与方向：用于本机视觉角度和反光变化，不用于定位。
  • 本地网络：用于连接你指定的媒体服务器和电视。

本 App 不请求麦克风录音权限。你可以在 iOS 设置中管理这些权限。

诊断与联系邮件

App 会在本机保存运行状态和部分诊断记录，用于排查问题；当前版本没有开发者自营的远程诊断上传接口。只有当你主动向下方邮箱发送反馈或附件时，相关内容和你的邮箱地址才会发送给开发者，用于处理你的请求。

数据管理与权利

你可以修改或退出媒体服务器连接。删除 App 会移除其应用目录内的数据，但系统 Keychain 中的连接资料或免费额度可能保留。服务器侧记录、第三方服务日志和 Apple 交易记录由相应服务管理，删除本机 App 不会自动删除这些记录。若你曾联系开发者，可以发邮件要求查阅或删除相关往来；开发者会依法处理你的请求。

政策变更

如本政策发生实质变更，会更新此页面的生效日期，并在必要时于 App 内提示。

Lumo Player Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Lumo Player (the app) is provided by the individual developer identified as the seller on its App Store listing. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and how you can control it.

Summary

  • No separate Lumo account is required. Connecting to your own media server requires that server’s account.
  • The current version contains no advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking SDKs, and does not request contacts or location access.
  • The app does not upload your media-server account, password, viewing history or library information to a developer-operated data server.

Your media server and on-device information

Your device sends your username, password and a randomly generated app device identifier directly to the server you enter. Subsequent requests use its access token. Playback sessions, progress and favorite changes may also be sent to that server. The server operator may receive your IP address and request times and handles them under its own policies.

Server addresses, accounts, access tokens and optional passwords are stored in the device’s Keychain for future connections, without Keychain synchronization. Catalogs, image caches, favorites, watch-later items, recent viewing and playback preferences are stored locally. The current version does not enable iCloud synchronization of these personal records. Device backup behavior depends on your iOS settings.

Image and name services

Library and title information primarily comes from your media server. If it returns TMDB image addresses, the image gallery may load images or thumbnails directly from image.tmdb.org. Movie and person name matching may send names, search terms and language to Wikidata (www.wikidata.org). These services receive information needed for a network connection, such as your IP address, and process it under their own policies. The app does not send your media-server login password to these image or name services.

Clipboard and TV pairing

The connection flow may attempt to recognize connection text in the clipboard. Choosing Copy Connection places connection information, potentially including a password, in the system clipboard. Share it only with recipients you trust.

TV pairing sends connection information, including your media-server address and access token, to the TV you select over an encrypted local-network connection. The current pairing payload excludes the media-server password. TV remote connection keys are stored in the device’s Keychain.

Purchases and daily preview

Apple processes subscriptions, lifetime purchases, payments and refunds. The app reads Apple-verified transactions and subscription status to determine unlimited-playback access, and never receives your payment card or payment method. The daily preview records the date and total actual playback time in the device’s Keychain. This record is not used for advertising or cross-app identification and does not sync between devices.

Device permissions

  • Photos (add only): requested when you choose to save an image; existing photos are not read.
  • Camera: used for TV pairing QR codes and device pose estimation when you enable spatial tracking. The current implementation does not save or upload camera images.
  • Motion: used locally for visual perspective and reflections, not location.
  • Local network: used to connect to your selected media server and TV.

The app does not request microphone recording access. You can manage these permissions in iOS Settings.

Diagnostics and email

The app stores some operating status and diagnostic information locally for troubleshooting. The current version has no developer-operated remote diagnostic-upload endpoint. Your email address, feedback and attachments reach the developer only when you choose to send an email, and are used to handle your request.

Data management and your rights

You can change or sign out of a media-server connection. Deleting the app removes data within its app directory, but connection information or preview allowance in the system Keychain may remain. Server records, third-party logs and Apple transaction records are managed by their respective services; deleting the app does not automatically remove them. If you have contacted the developer, you can email to request access to or deletion of that correspondence. Requests will be handled as required by applicable law.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected in this page’s effective date and, where appropriate, communicated in the app.
